========== MISTAKE 1 — histogram_quantile without 'by (le)' ==========

The correct form aggregates buckets while KEEPING the le label:

--- correct: sum by (le)
promql: histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket{job="slo-demo",path="/api"}[5m]))) 
  (no labels)  =>  198.61ms

Dropping 'by (le)' removes the only label the function can interpolate over:

--- wrong: bare sum
promql: histogram_quantile(0.99, sum(rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket{job="slo-demo",path="/api"}[5m]))) 
  (empty result)

========== MISTAKE 2 — rate(sum(...)) instead of sum(rate(...)) ==========

Correct — rate each pod's counter, then add the per-second results:

--- correct: sum(rate(...))
promql: sum(rate(http_requests_total{job="slo-demo",path="/api"}[5m])) 
  (no labels)  =>  584.015

Wrong — adding raw counters first. It happens to look plausible while every pod
is up; the moment one restarts, the summed counter drops and rate() reads the
reset as a real decrease. Same shape, silently wrong exactly during an incident:

--- wrong: rate(sum(...))
promql: rate(sum(http_requests_total{job="slo-demo",path="/api"})[5m:15s]) 
  (no labels)  =>  589.232
