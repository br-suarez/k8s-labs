========== FIX 1 — correct the nginx directive, then roll the Deployment ==========

$ kubectl apply -f fixed/01-crashloop-fixed.yaml
configmap/nginx-config configured

A ConfigMap change does not restart consumers, and a subPath mount does not
even refresh the file in the running container. The rollout is mandatory:

$ kubectl rollout restart deploy/web-crashloop -n k8s-refresher
deployment.apps/web-crashloop restarted

$ kubectl rollout status deploy/web-crashloop -n k8s-refresher --timeout=120s
Waiting for deployment "web-crashloop" rollout to finish: 1 old replicas are pending termination...
Waiting for deployment "web-crashloop" rollout to finish: 1 old replicas are pending termination...
Waiting for deployment "web-crashloop" rollout to finish: 1 old replicas are pending termination...
deployment "web-crashloop" successfully rolled out
